About Us 🍌🍪

We provide freshly baked, affordable, and delicious treats for every Filipino home.


Our Store

Homemade Cookies and Banana Bread is a home-based bakery located in San Ildefonso Magalang, Pampanga. We help customers find tasty baked goods for daily snacks, special occasions, or sharing with family and friends.


Why Customers Trust Us

We focus on using quality ingredients, consistent taste, fair prices, simple ordering, and friendly customer support.


Our Goal

Our goal is to make ordering homemade baked goods easier, more accessible, and more enjoyable for customers.
